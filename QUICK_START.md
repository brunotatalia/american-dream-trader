# ⚡ Quick Start Guide

```bash
cd "/Users/yossi/AMERICAN DREAM TRADE/american-dream-trader"

```

Open **http://localhost:5173** in your browser.

## 🎮 First 5 Minutes

1. **Choose Era**: Select "The Roaring Twenties"
2. **Skip Tutorial**: Or watch if you want
3. **Dashboard**: You start with $500
4. **Go to Jobs**: Click briefcase icon (sidebar or bottom nav)
5. **Pick Factory Worker**: Click the tab
6. **Click "Start Work"**: Play the Assembly Line mini-game
7. **Earn $25-35**: Based on your performance
8. **Go to Trading**: Click chart icon
9. **Buy 5 shares of GM**: At $32 each (costs $170)
10. **Click Resume**: Top bar, watch time advance
11. **See prices move**: GM might go to $34-35
12. **Sell for profit**: Or hold longer!

**Congrats! You just played the game.** 🎉

## 💡 Quick Tips

### Making Money Fast
- **Jobs**: Reliable income ($20-60 per game)
- **Stocks**: Buy low, sell high (risky)
- **Real Estate**: Need $1K-5K down, passive income
- **Casino**: High risk, house edge (entertainment only)

### Best Starting Moves
1. Work 5-10 jobs to build $250-500
2. Buy cheap volatile stocks (Chrysler $28, Pfizer $28)
3. Resume time on Fast speed
4. Watch for 10-20% gains
5. Sell and reinvest
6. Repeat until $5K
7. Buy first property!

### Controls
- **Pause**: Top bar button or click time speed dropdown
- **Speed**: Slow (1 day/min), Normal (1 day/30s), Fast (1 day/12s)
- **Navigate**: Sidebar (desktop) or bottom bar (mobile)
- **Help**: Question mark icon in top bar

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### System Requirements
- **Node.js**: 20+ recommended (works on 18 with warnings)
- **Browser**: Modern Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge
- **RAM**: 2GB+ recommended
- **Storage**: ~50MB for game files

### Known Behaviors
- Prices update every game day
- Rent collects every 30 game days
- Events trigger randomly (save often!)
- Casino has house edge (expect to lose long-term)
- Time stops when modals are open

### Troubleshooting
- **Connection Refused**: Run `npm run dev` first
- **Blank Screen**: Check browser console (F12)
- **Slow Performance**: Use Normal or Slow speed
- **Lost Progress**: Check localStorage isn't cleared
