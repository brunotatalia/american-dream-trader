### "The $500 to $10,000 Challenge"

**Starting Position:**
- Cash: $500
- Net Worth: $500
- Goal: Reach $10,000

**Week 1 (Days 1-7):**

**Week 2-4 (Days 8-30):**
- Work Secretary 3 times: +$120
- Chrysler rises to $35 (+25%): Portfolio now $525
- Sell Chrysler: +$515
- Cash: $830
- Buy Brooklyn Studio ($5K, $1K down): -$1,000
- Cash: -$170... need more jobs!

**Month 2 (Days 31-60):**
- Work 8 more jobs: +$200
- Cash: $30
- Collect rent: +$50
- Cash: $80
- Save and wait for rent...

**Actually, Let's Revise:**

**Smart Strategy:**
1. Work 15 jobs over 2 weeks: $375 earned
2. Total cash: $500 + $375 = $875
3. Buy 25 shares of GM at $32 = $800 + $10 fee
4. Cash remaining: $65
5. Resume time, speed = Fast
6. GM rises 2% daily average
7. After 30 days: GM = $32 × 1.02^30 = $58
8. Portfolio value: 25 × $58 = $1,450
9. Sell GM: +$1,440 after fees
10. Cash: $65 + $1,440 = $1,505
11. Buy Brooklyn Studio ($1K down)
12. Cash: $505, Property: $5K value
13. Net Worth: $505 + $5,000 = $5,505
14. Collect $50 rent monthly
15. Keep working + trading + compounding
16. Reach $10K in 6-12 months! ✅

## 🎓 Lessons Learned

### Development Best Practices

1. **Start with Foundation**: Time spent on setup pays dividends
2. **Design System First**: Reusable components = faster features
3. **State Management Matters**: Zustand made complex state simple
4. **Document as You Go**: READMEs write themselves if incremental
5. **Lint Early**: Catches issues before they compound
6. **Manual Testing**: Play your own game frequently
7. **User Perspective**: Think like a player, not a dev

### Game Design Insights

1. **Balance is Critical**: Earnings vs. costs vs. progression
2. **Feedback Loops Matter**: Toasts make actions feel impactful
3. **Multiple Paths**: Players like options (work vs. trade vs. gamble)
4. **Risk/Reward**: Must be tunable and clear
5. **Progression Curves**: Start gentle, ramp up complexity
6. **Historical Flavor**: Narratives add immersion
7. **UI Clarity**: Players shouldn't think about controls
