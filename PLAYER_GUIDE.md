## 💼 Making Money Through Work

### How Jobs Work

1. **Go to Jobs Section** (briefcase icon)
2. **Browse Available Jobs** (filtered by era)
3. **Select a Job** (tabs at top)
4. **Read Job Details** (pay, difficulty, requirements)
5. **Click "Start Work"** to launch mini-game
6. **Play the Mini-Game** (30-60 seconds)
7. **Collect Earnings** based on performance

### Mini-Game Tips

#### Assembly Line (Factory/Dock Worker)
- **Goal**: Click the color shown at top
- **Duration**: 30 seconds
- **Strategy**: Stay focused, build combos
- **Bonus**: 95%+ accuracy = +25% pay

#### Typing Challenge (Secretary)
- **Goal**: Type sentences exactly as shown
- **Duration**: 60 seconds
- **Strategy**: Accuracy over speed initially
- **Bonus**: 98% accuracy + 40 WPM = +25% pay

#### Math Quiz (Bank Teller/Accountant)
- **Goal**: Solve arithmetic problems
- **Duration**: 45 seconds
- **Strategy**: Mental math, no calculator!
- **Bonus**: 95%+ accuracy = +25% pay

#### Delivery Route (Driver/Bootlegger)
- **Goal**: Visit locations in efficient order
- **Duration**: 40 seconds
- **Strategy**: Plan route like traveling salesman
- **Bonus**: 95%+ efficiency = +25% pay

#### Construction (Construction Worker)
- **Goal**: Stack 10 blocks by matching shown block
- **Duration**: 35 seconds
- **Strategy**: Speed and precision
- **Bonus**: Perfect completion = +25% pay

### Job Strategy

## 🎰 Casino & Entertainment

### When to Gamble
- **Small Bets**: Entertainment, not investment
- **House Edge**: Casino always wins long-term
- **Quick Cash**: Risky but fast
- **Never Risk Essentials**: Don't gamble rent money!

### Slot Machine
- **How to Play**: Set bet, click Spin
- **Symbols**: 🍒 (common) to 7️⃣ (rare)
- **Payouts**: 10x to 100x your bet
- **House Edge**: ~5%
- **Strategy**: Set budget, stick to it

### Roulette
- **How to Play**: Select bet type (Red/Black, Dozens, etc.), set amount, spin
- **Best Odds**: Outside bets (Red/Black, Even/Odd) = 48.6% win
- **Higher Risk**: Dozens = 32% win but 2:1 payout
- **House Edge**: 2.7% (European single-zero)
- **Strategy**: Avoid chasing losses

### Blackjack
- **How to Play**: Bet, get dealt cards, hit or stand, beat dealer to 21
- **Basic Strategy**: Stand on 17+, hit on 11 or less, judge 12-16
- **Blackjack**: 21 on first two cards pays 3:2
- **Dealer**: Always draws to 17
- **House Edge**: ~0.5% (best odds in casino!)
- **Strategy**: Learn when to hit vs. stand
